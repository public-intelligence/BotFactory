# Bot Factory Content Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-11-25
**Last updated:** 2026-11-25
**Published by:** Center for Public Intelligence (CPI)

## 1. Scope

This Content Policy governs the content handled by the **Bot Factory** software platform across three categories:

1. **Knowledge-base content:** Plain-text files (`.txt`), web pages, and URL lists uploaded or indexed by deploying institutions as approved sources for their assistants.
2. **Visitor inputs:** Text messages and spoken queries (transcribed to text) submitted by members of the public interacting with a published assistant.
3. **Assistant outputs:** Text and spoken answers generated by an assistant in response to visitor inputs.

## 2. Responsibility

- **Deploying institutions** are responsible for the legality, accuracy, and appropriateness of all knowledge-base sources they upload, the prompts and tools they configure, and the published behavior of their assistants.
- **CPI** is responsible for the Bot Factory software and for enforcing this policy on any hosted or pilot instance operated by CPI.

## 3. Prohibited Content

The following content is strictly prohibited in knowledge bases, prompts, assistant configurations, and (to the extent controllable through moderation and takedown processes) assistant outputs:

- **Illegal content:** Any content that violates applicable local or international law in the deploying institution's jurisdiction.
- **Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and exploitation:** Any sexualized content involving minors or content that facilitates the exploitation or abuse of children.
- **Harassment, hate speech, and violence:** Content that threatens, harasses, incites violence, or promotes unlawful discrimination against individuals or protected groups.
- **Unauthorized personal data:** Publishing or indexing personal data without a valid legal basis, or attempting to expose private credentials, government IDs, or confidential records.
- **Malware and deceptive links:** Malicious software, phishing links, or instructions designed to compromise systems or defraud users.

## 4. Content Controls and Identification

Bot Factory combines institutional governance with technical controls to prevent harmful or inaccurate content:

- **Curated institutional knowledge bases:** Only authorized users with the `kb:update` permission can upload `.txt` files or add web-page URLs. Institutions can inspect, refresh, and audit their indexed sources from the admin panel.
- **Required-grounding mode (`GroundingGate`):** Institutions can enable required-grounding mode on any assistant. In this mode, the assistant must consult approved knowledge-base sources before answering, and a deterministic verification gate strips unverified figures, monetary amounts, phone numbers, email addresses, and URLs that do not appear in the consulted sources or knowledge base.
- **Safety filtering on inputs and outputs:** When using the default language-model provider (Google Gemini), the provider's safety filters block prohibited requests and harmful outputs, and Bot Factory logs each refusal (`llm_no_answer` / `GeminiSafety`) for institutional review.
- **AI transparency:** Published assistants identify themselves as automated AI assistants in their greeting and interface.

## 5. Reporting Inappropriate or Illegal Content

- **Current reporting process:** Visitors and third parties who encounter inaccurate, inappropriate, or unlawful content from a Bot Factory assistant may report it directly to the institution operating the assistant. For instances operated by CPI, or to escalate reports involving CPI-hosted deployments, contact **Melissa Muñoz** at `melissa@publicintelligence.ai`.
- **Planned in-product reporting:** A built-in **"Report this answer"** control in the public chat and embeddable widget, connected to a review queue in the institutional admin panel, is planned so visitors can flag problematic responses in one click.

## 6. Content Removal and Enforcement

- **Institutional removal controls:** Authorized administrators can immediately delete a knowledge-base document (`kb:delete` permission), modify an assistant's instructions, or unpublish an assistant (`bot:publish` permission) from the admin panel.
- **CPI-operated instances:** Removal or enforcement requests must be submitted with a **solid factual or legal basis and supporting documentation**. CPI will review substantiated reports of prohibited content on instances it operates and remove or disable offending content or assistants within **48 hours** (or immediately for severe harms such as CSAM or credible threats of violence).
- **Lawful requests:** CPI and deploying institutions will cooperate with valid legal orders and law-enforcement requests in accordance with applicable law.

## 7. Minors and Youth Safety

Bot Factory assistants are designed for the general public (adults aged 18 and older) and **do not permit user-to-user interaction** (visitors interact solely with an institutional assistant, never with other visitors).

- Institutions operating assistants that may be accessed by minors must configure appropriate system prompts, enable required-grounding mode, avoid collecting unnecessary personal data, and display age-appropriate guidance.

## 8. Policy Review

CPI reviews this Content Policy at least once a year, or sooner when material technical changes (such as in-product reporting tools or new moderation mechanisms) are introduced.
